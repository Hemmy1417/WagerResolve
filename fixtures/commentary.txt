About the reference rate

The reference rate matters to households and businesses because it feeds
through to mortgages, savings and the cost of commercial credit. Our
economists publish commentary on the direction of travel, the pressures the
committee weighs, and how conditions compare with previous cycles.

Readers often ask how quickly a change reaches the wider economy. The honest
answer is that it varies: some contracts reprice immediately, others only at
renewal, and sentiment can move well before any instrument does.

This page carries no figures. For the published series, consult the daily
rate table. This document is synthetic and exists only to exercise a test
suite.
